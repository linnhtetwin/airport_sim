The logo-free A320, A350, A380, B737, and B787 models are used in Airport Ops Sim.
They are scaled and oriented for the simulation. No changes were made to their mesh geometry.

Boeing 757-200 (B752.glb), Boeing 767-300 (B763.glb), and Embraer 190
(E190.glb) neutral models: Bluebell CSL contributors, converted to glTF by
SDRangel and repackaged by Clear Aviation Tracking without airline textures.
Geometry is released under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
https://clearaviationtracking.com/map-credits/
https://github.com/srcejon/sdrangel-3d-models/blob/main/LICENSE

E190.glb also stands in for the closely related E170, E175, E195, and E195-E2
aircraft, fitted to each type's length and wingspan where applicable.
