The logo-free A320, A350, A380, B737, and B787 models are used in Airport Ops Sim.
They are scaled and oriented for the simulation. No changes were made to their mesh geometry.

Boeing 757-200 (B752.glb), Boeing 767-300 (B763.glb), and Embraer 190
(E190.glb) neutral models: Bluebell CSL contributors, converted to glTF by
SDRangel and repackaged by Clear Aviation Tracking without airline textures.
Geometry is released under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
https://clearaviationtracking.com/map-credits/
https://github.com/srcejon/sdrangel-3d-models/blob/main/LICENSE

E190.glb also stands in for the closely related E170, E175, E195, and E195-E2
aircraft, fitted to each type's length and wingspan where applicable.

Bombardier CRJ900 (CRJ9.glb), Dash 8 Q400 (DH8D.glb), Boeing 747-400 (B744.glb)
and Cessna 172 (C172.glb) neutral models: Bluebell CSL contributors, converted to glTF by SDRangel, with
the airline textures replaced by one neutral material for Airport Ops Sim and
the spinning-propeller blur discs removed. Geometry is released under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
https://github.com/srcejon/sdrangel-3d-models/tree/59d1ee24ebc2c24343afb54b26928f965a625140/BB_Jets_png/CRJ9
https://github.com/srcejon/sdrangel-3d-models/tree/59d1ee24ebc2c24343afb54b26928f965a625140/BB_Props_png/DH8D
https://github.com/srcejon/sdrangel-3d-models/tree/59d1ee24ebc2c24343afb54b26928f965a625140/BB_GA_png/C172
https://github.com/srcejon/sdrangel-3d-models/tree/59d1ee24ebc2c24343afb54b26928f965a625140/BB_Boeing_png/B744

CRJ9.glb also stands in for the CRJ100/200, CRJ700 and CRJ1000, and DH8D.glb
for the Dash 8-100, -200 and -300, each fitted to its type's length and
wingspan. B744.glb is used for every 747, stretched to the longer 747-8 (no free
747-8 model exists). C172.glb stands in for other light aircraft, such as the
Piper PA-28.

The game paints airline liveries (tail, cheatline, belly) on these neutral
models at run time; the files themselves stay plain.

Bell 206 JetRanger (B06.glb) neutral model: Bluebell CSL contributors, CC0 1.0,
converted from the X-Plane OBJ8 model to glTF for Airport Ops Sim (parked pose:
rotors stopped, no pilots or light cones). It stands in for the Bell 407 and
other helicopters:
